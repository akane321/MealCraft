# Full default: plan wall time

> Scope: the cases of `latest.md`, one machine. The report's run warms the candidate cache; the warm runs are the same plan again. Median and slowest of the warm runs. ADR-0079 sets how long is acceptable by how hard the request is: a simple request should still come back within about 4 s, a complex one may take up to about 60 s, and only past that is a case marked over. A case is never cut short to meet either figure, and no feature is dropped to make one faster.

Machine: Intel64 Family 6 Model 198 Stepping 2, GenuineIntel (Windows). The code commit is in `timing.json` beside this file.

Over ~60 s: four-with-vegetarian, six-tight-budget, seven-with-young-children.

A case with `-` ran once, the report's run: a warm run of it takes as long again (some take almost an hour), and the report run is read against the guidance in its place.

- `four-with-vegetarian` (over ~60 s): one beam search over every combination of every role's usual candidates: since task 15 removed the cap of 16,384 combinations a meal (owner, 2026-10-22), a meal checks every one of them, about 0.9 million in the first 400 s of a profiled run, and the time went from about 19 s to about 2 min. building each meal's dish combinations and checking every one as a real meal (`meal_beam._meal_options` -> `meal_permitted` -> `meal_composition.real_meal_issues`) is essentially the whole profiled window, and `member_coverage.member_can_eat` (asked again for every member, dish and combination, with nothing cached per member and recipe) about a quarter of it (21 million calls). Upgrade path, none of it changing a plan: cache the member checks per member and recipe, and a recipe's meal role.

- `six-tight-budget` (over ~60 s): four whole-week beam searches instead of one (the ranked search keeps no week filled with no dish twice under the budget, so the three budget-led searches of `product_path.limit_led` run too), and a variety pass over 16 starts that no longer stops at two seconds (ADR-0080). Since task 15 removed the cap on a meal's combinations (owner, 2026-10-22) every one of the four searches checks every combination of every role's usual candidates, and the time went from about 2 min to about 15 min. In the first 1,200 s of a profiled run (two searches), building each meal's dish combinations and checking every one as a real meal (`meal_beam._meal_options` -> `meal_permitted` -> `meal_composition.real_meal_issues`) is essentially all of it (2.3 million combinations) and `member_coverage.member_can_eat` (asked again for every member, dish and combination, with nothing cached per member and recipe) about 28% (68 million calls); the variety pass was not reached, so its share since 2026-10-21 (about 50% of the run when each search was capped) is not re-measured. Upgrade path, none of it changing a plan: share the meal checks between the four searches (they do not depend on the ranking), cache the member checks per member and recipe, and make the variety pass faster. The owner accepted this case's time (ADR-0079 section 5, ADR-0080): over the line is a known exception, not a failure.

- `two-no-oven-ten-days` (within the guidance for a complex request): one beam search over ten days and two shopping trips, about two-thirds of the run; building each meal's dish combinations and checking every one as a real meal (`meal_beam._meal_options` -> `meal_permitted` -> `meal_composition.real_meal_issues`) is about 42%, and reading the candidates about 15%; the plan has 30 slots of combinations to build.

- `seven-with-young-children` (over ~60 s): one beam search over seven-dish dinners for a household of seven. Since task 15 removed the cap of 16,384 combinations a meal (owner, 2026-10-22) each dinner checks every combination of every role's usual candidates, 1.4 million in the first 600 s of a profiled run, and the time went from about 9 s to about 48 min. building each meal's dish combinations and checking every one as a real meal (`meal_beam._meal_options` -> `meal_permitted` -> `meal_composition.real_meal_issues`) is essentially the whole profiled window; in it `member_coverage.member_can_eat` (asked again for every member, dish and combination, with nothing cached per member and recipe) is about a quarter (44 million calls) and the recipe's meal role (`meal_composition.meal_role_of` -> `unreviewed`, asked again for every dish of every combination) more than half. Upgrade path, none of it changing a plan: cache a recipe's meal role and the member checks per member and recipe, and prune a role's candidates against the meal's hard limits before taking their product.
