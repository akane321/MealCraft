# What the first held-out run shows

The numbers are in `latest.md`, generated by the runner. This file reads them.
The live-model arms (A, B, D) have not run, so nothing here compares an agent
with anything.

## The beam does not plan against a budget, and the exact solver does

`O1` (gold constraints, meal beam) fails 6 of the 40 episodes. `O2` (gold
constraints, CP-SAT) fails none. Every one of the six is an episode with a
weekly budget: `md-ho-005`, `md-ho-008`, `md-ho-019`, `md-ho-020`, `md-ho-021`
and `md-ho-022`. On each, the beam returns "no validated plan" although the
set's own proof carries a witness week within the budget.

The cause is in the search, not in the constraints: the meal beam scores dishes
and never looks at cost, so the plans it keeps are the ones it likes, and the
validator then finds them over budget. CP-SAT holds the budget as a constraint
and finds a plan every time.

This is the clearest separation the set produced, and it is a real product
weakness: the product path runs the same beam, so a household with a tight
weekly budget can be told no plan was found when one exists.

**It is not fixed here.** A fix is a parameter or algorithm change, and it may
be developed only against the developer set (`ADR-0020` section 2 as amended by
`ADR-0029`). Tuning it against these six episodes would spend the held-out set.

## The rule arms fail mostly at understanding, not at planning

`C` (rules + beam) reaches 17/40, `E` (Strong Rule-only) 17/40 and `F` (greedy)
21/40, and their failure mechanisms are led by `status_matches_class`,
`no_fabricated_plan` and `conflict_named`: they answer with a plan where the
episode is infeasible or needs a question. The rule parser reads none of the
budgets and none of the conflicts, so every infeasible and most clarification
episodes are answered with a plan. Their planning failures (allergens,
exclusions, time, repetition requests) are a smaller share.

That `F`, the weakest planner, scores highest of the three is a symptom of the
same thing: strict success rewards a valid plan, and `F` fabricates one for a
few episodes whose constraints it never read but happened not to break.

## Variety, reported beside success

Over the 40 episodes: `E` 16.3 distinct recipes with no adjacent repeats, `C`
12.5, the gold arms about 11.9, and `F` 2.37 with 426 adjacent repeats. The
gold arms repeat more than `E` because the constrained episodes leave them few
eligible dishes, and repetition is only a failure when the household asked
about it (protocol section 4).

## Solve time

Medians are all under a fifth of a second. `O2`'s 95th percentile is 84.5 s and
its slowest episode 141.7 s, within the 300 s limit; it proved optimality on 15
of 40. The rest returned the best plan found within the limit.
